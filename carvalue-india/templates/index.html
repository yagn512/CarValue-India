How it works

  1. Dataset: real Indian used-car listings from CarDekho.
  2. Cleaning: missing values, duplicates, impossible values and extreme price outliers are removed.
  3. Models: Linear Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest are trained on 80% of the data.
  4. Selection: the model with the lowest error on the unseen 20% test data is used for predictions.
